# Example: Nova Clinics scope

Project: Booking site
Client: Nova Clinics
Owner (you): Ali Hamza
Date: 12 Mar 2026

## Goal (one sentence)

Patients book a slot on the clinic site, and the front desk sees one list instead of a WhatsApp thread.

## In scope

- Public booking form: service, doctor, date, slot
- Admin list with status (booked, done, no-show)
- One SMS reminder, 24 hours before
- CSV export
- Deploy on the clinic domain

## Out of scope

- Patient login
- Medical records
- Payments
- More than one clinic location
- A custom mobile app

## Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Format |
|---|-------------|--------|
| 1 | Booking page | Live URL |
| 2 | Admin appointment list | Login for the owner |
| 3 | SMS reminder | One provider, tested |

## Success checks

- [ ] A test patient can book a real slot
