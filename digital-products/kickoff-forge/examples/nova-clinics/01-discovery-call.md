# Example: Nova Clinics discovery

This is a filled sample. Your blank sheet is `01-discovery-call.md`.

Client: Nova Clinics
Date: 12 Mar 2026
Channel: call

## Problem in their words

Patients book by WhatsApp. The front desk double-books and loses follow-ups. They want a simple booking site with SMS reminders before they pay for a full medical records system.

## Who is hurt if this stays broken?

Front desk (chaos), doctors (no-shows), patients (wait and mistrust).

## What done looks like in 30 days

A live booking page, an admin list of appointments, an SMS reminder 24 hours before, and a CSV export for the owner.

## Must-haves (max 5)

1. Public booking form (service, doctor, slot)
2. Admin list of appointments
3. SMS reminder through one provider
4. Logo and two brand colors
5. Deploy on their domain

## Nice-to-haves (parked)

- Patient accounts
- Full medical records
- Online payments

## Constraints

- Budget band: $1.5k to $2.5k for the MVP
- Deadline: before the busy season, soft date
- Who approves work: clinic owner, Dr. Sara
- Existing stack / hosting: none they want to keep
- Brand / design assets ready? logo yes, photos partial

## Risks I heard

- Owner may ask for records features mid-build
- SMS provider account is not created yet
